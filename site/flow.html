25 years

Twenty-five years of the world's biggest companies, in one picture

Every company is a line. Time runs left to right, rank runs up the side. Lines climb into the top of the map, sink out of the bottom edge, and new lines appear when a company first reports. Below it, the same years by business idea: which kinds of business took a bigger share, and which faded.

How to read it. The vertical axis is rank among all firms that reported that measure that year (1 at the top, on a log scale). A line stops at the bottom edge when the company drops out of view and climbs back in when it returns; an open circle marks a company's first reported year. Hover any line to see what the company does, when it grew and when it fell. For the same story over ten years and a fixed set of firms, see Change over time.
