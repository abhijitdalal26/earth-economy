The Forbes 2000, year by year

Ten years of the biggest listed companies on earth, scrubbable. Drag the year and watch the list change under you: who is on top, which business ideas are growing, how much of the revenue a handful of firms hold, and which companies climbed.

Read this before quoting anything here. These are today's survivors: the 2,000 companies on the Forbes Global 2000 (2026) list, each with up to ten years of reported financials. Firms that collapsed — Credit Suisse, Evergrande, Wirecard — are absent from the start, so every growth rate on this page is flattered. Figures are the companies' own reported fiscal years (revenue and profit in USD; headcount is patchy before 2019). Market value is a single 2026 snapshot, so it is not shown over time.

      How this was built. The companies are the Forbes Global 2000 (2026) list; the history is each firm's own reported revenue, profit and headcount for fiscal years 2016 to 2025 (data/global2000_2026_history.csv). Ranks are computed against every firm that reported that metric that year, so early years rank fewer companies. Sectors follow the 13-sector taxonomy used across the site. Figures are snapshots of reported accounts, not a like-for-like index.
